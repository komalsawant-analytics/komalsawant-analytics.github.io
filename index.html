Finding 01all channels · 6 months

The bestseller is not the best earner.

Vitamin C Serum₹18.7L rev₹4.2L profit
Mineral Sunscreen₹10.2L rev₹5.5L profit

The serum brings 18.6% of all revenue, but the sunscreen earns more profit on 55% of the sales. The serum averages a 22% website discount, and its Meta spend equals 65% of its Shopify revenue.

RecommendationMove part of the serum's ad budget to the sunscreen and cap serum discount codes at 15%.

Finding 02BeautyMart

Lip tints come back 1 in 4 times.

Lip tints27.3%
Rest of range4.9%

Returned lipstick can't be resold. Three lip tints with ₹6.9L of revenue keep only a 4.5% margin after ads, while the rest of the range averages over 30%.

RecommendationAdd a shade guide and real-skin swatches to the listing, or sell lip tints only where returns are low.

Finding 03June sale week

The marketplace sale bought volume, not profit.

Normal weeks36.7%
Sale week26.1%

Margin before ads fell by 10 points during sale week, because discounts reached 35% on top of the 25% marketplace commission.

RecommendationJoin the next sale with a curated set of high-margin products, not the full range.

Business rules in the profit maths
  • Website payment gateway is 2% of net sales; courier costs ₹70 per order, split across its lines.
  • A customer return refunds the sale and costs ₹80 in reverse pickup. Opened skincare is written off.
  • Marketplace RTO parcels and Amazon returns marked sellable go back to stock, so product cost is recovered.
  • Meta ads drive website traffic, so ad spend is charged to Shopify. Brand and retargeting spend is shared by each product's website revenue that month.
  • Amazon storage fees are a channel cost. Amazon and marketplace ad spend were not in the files, so those margins are before their own ads.
